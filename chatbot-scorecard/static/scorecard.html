Chatbot Scorecard

This scorecard is intended to help measure how your SRH chatbot is performing compared to industry best practice. By filling out high/medium/low below based on the Metric Scoring Cheat Sheet, you can identify areas of strength and where investment is needed to improve.

The scorecard measures chatbots across three key dimensions: Product design and development, User experience, and Forward-looking metrics.
